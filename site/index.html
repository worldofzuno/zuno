Shipping & Returns

Shipping Terms

Our shipping terms apply to deliveries within Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. We do not currently ship internationally.

Unless otherwise stated, all items are usually available from stock and ready for dispatch within two business days. If this is not the case, we will contact the customer. Orders are shipped via Swiss Post. Once the order is ready for shipment, the customer will be notified by email.

If the customer is not at home at the time of delivery and no specific delivery instructions have been provided, the package may be left in front of the door or with a neighbour in the same building. For this purpose, the customer’s address and telephone number must be provided. Delivery is made at the customer’s risk and expense, including in cases of partial deliveries.

Shipping Costs

We charge a flat shipping fee of CHF 7 per order within Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Shipping is free for orders of CHF 45 or more. The shipping fee includes packaging, postage and handling.

If not all items in an order are available immediately, any additional shipping costs for subsequent deliveries will be covered by us. We aim to ship all items together whenever possible; if this is not feasible, the order may be held until all items are available.

Returns

Swiss law does not provide a statutory right of withdrawal for online purchases. As a courtesy, ZUNO voluntarily offers the following:

If a customer receives a damaged or defective item, we will provide a replacement or a full refund once the item has been returned to us. Please report any damaged or incorrect items within 14 days of receiving your order.

Used items, or products damaged by the customer, are excluded from returns. Opened coffee is excluded from exchange or return for hygiene and safety reasons and will always be charged.

If a customer places an order that was unwanted, incorrect or made by mistake, the return shipping costs (postage and return label) must be fully covered by the customer.

Return Period

Returns must be made within 14 days of receiving the goods.
