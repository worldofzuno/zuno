Privacy Policy

At ZUNO, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you visit our website and make purchases from our online store.

What Information Do We Collect?

  • Personal Information: When you place an order or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect personal details such as your name, email address, shipping address, and payment information.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use our website, including your IP address, browser type, and device information.

How Do We Use Your Information?

  • To process and fulfill your orders.
  • To communicate with you about your order, including delivery status and promotions.
  • To improve our website and enhance your shopping experience.
  • To send you newsletters and marketing emails, if you have subscribed.

How Do We Protect Your Information?

We implement a variety of security measures to ensure the safety of your personal information when you place an order or enter, submit, or access your personal information.

Third-Party Disclosures

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. However, we may share information with trusted third parties to help us operate our website, conduct business, or provide services to you.

Your Rights

Shipping Policy

Shipping Terms

Our shipping terms apply to deliveries within Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. Currently, we do not ship internationally.

Unless otherwise stated, all items are usually available from stock and ready for dispatch within two business days. If this is not the case, we will contact the customer. Orders are shipped via Swiss Post. Once the order is ready for shipment, the customer will be notified by email.

Items that are not in stock will be ordered from the manufacturer, provided they are available. As soon as the items arrive at our warehouse, we will inform the customer by email or phone and ship the order immediately.

If the customer is not at home at the time of delivery and no specific delivery instructions have been provided, the package may be left in front of the door or with a neighbour living in the same building. For this purpose, the customer’s address and telephone number must be provided.

Delivery is made at the customer’s risk and expense, including in cases of partial deliveries. Customers can view their order history at any time using their personal account login.

Shipping Costs

We deliver orders directly to your home and charge a flat shipping fee of CHF 4.90 per order within Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein.

Shipping is free for orders over CHF 45.

The shipping fee includes packaging, postage and handling costs.

If not all items in an order are available immediately, any additional shipping costs for subsequent deliveries will be covered by us. However, we aim to ship all items together whenever possible. If this is not feasible, the order may be held until all items are available.

Legal

Return Policy

If a customer receives a damaged or defective item, we will provide a replacement or a full refund once the damaged or defective product has been returned to us.

Used items or products damaged by the customer are excluded from the return policy. Ground coffee is excluded from exchange or return and will always be charged.

If a customer places an order that was unwanted, incorrect or made by mistake, the return shipping costs (postage and return label) must be fully covered by the customer.

Return Period

Returns must be made within 10 days of receiving the goods.
