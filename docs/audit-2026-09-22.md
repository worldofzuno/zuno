## Implementation integrity verdict — PASS

The page expresses a system that could not be lifted onto another product.
Felix Titling paired with self-hosted Inter, a seven-token brand palette,
real product photography cut to alpha, a hand-written CSP carrying
per-script SHA-256 hashes, and contrast measured rather than assumed.
Seventeen design tokens carry the colour, easing and timing vocabulary, and
they are used consistently.

The detector's seven findings do not contradict this. Four are
`overused-font` for Inter, which is the deliberate brand sans, not a
default reached for. One is `bounce-easing` on a single token used in one
place. Two are real and listed below.

## Findings by severity

### [P1] Text at 200% clips the navigation

- **Location**: `site/index.html` — `.nav__links`, `.menu ul li a`, `.icon-btn`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Measured**: at 390px with `html { font-size: 200% }`, nav items reach
  `right: 409px` against a 390px viewport — 19px past the edge. At a 195px
  viewport (200% browser zoom on a 390px device) the document overflows by
  40px and 10 elements are clipped. `body { overflow-x: hidden }` hides the
  scrollbar, so the overflow is lost rather than reachable.
- **Not affected**: SC 1.4.10 Reflow passes — at 320px the overflow is 0px
  and nothing is clipped.
- **Impact**: a reader who enlarges text loses the menu labels entirely;
  there is no scrollbar to recover them.
- **Standard**: WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.4 Resize Text (AA)
- **Recommendation**: let the nav row wrap below ~240px instead of holding
  one line, and drop `overflow-x: hidden` in favour of fixing the source of
  the overflow so nothing can be silently clipped.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable adapt`

### [P2] `color-scheme` is undeclared

- **Location**: `site/index.html` — `:root`
- **Category**: Theming
- **Measured**: `grep -c color-scheme` → 0, against 19 form controls.
- **Impact**: the browser renders native UI for a light page — scrollbars,
  date and select popups, autofill backgrounds and spellcheck underlines
  all arrive light on a page committed to black. Autofill in particular
  paints a pale yellow over the checkout fields.
- **Recommendation**: `color-scheme: dark` on `:root`. One line.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] Two `transition: height` rules animate layout

- **Location**: `site/index.html:235` (`.menu`), `site/index.html:434` (`.line`)
- **Category**: Performance
- **Impact**: height is a layout property; animating it reflows everything
  below on every frame. `.menu` is the mobile nav dropdown, so this runs on
  the lowest-powered devices. `.line` collapses a cart row on removal, so
  with several items each removal reflows the rows beneath it.
- **Recommendation**: `grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr` on a wrapper, which
  animates on the compositor and needs no measured height.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable optimize`

### [P2] `#16150e` sits outside the token set

- **Location**: `site/index.html:258`, `:276`, `:550`
- **Category**: Theming
- **Measured**: every other brand colour is a token in `:root`; this one
  value — the ink used on gold surfaces — is written literally three times.
- **Impact**: the gold button, the selected option pill and the skip link
  drift apart the moment one is edited.
- **Recommendation**: add `--on-gold: #16150e` and reference it.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable extract`

### [P2] No `<nav>` landmark

- **Location**: `site/index.html` — `header.nav`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Measured**: `document.querySelectorAll('nav').length` → 0;
  `[role=navigation]` → 0. Landmarks present: header, main, footer.
- **Impact**: screen-reader users cannot jump to navigation by landmark.
  The skip link covers the common case, so this is a convenience gap rather
  than a barrier.
- **Standard**: WCAG 2.2 SC 1.3.6 (AAA) — informative at AA
- **Recommendation**: wrap `ul.nav__links` in `<nav aria-label="Primary">`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### [P3] `--ease-back` overshoots

- **Location**: `site/index.html:90` (token), `:224` (only use)
- **Category**: Implementation integrity
- **Measured**: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`, applied to one
  transform — the cart badge scaling from `0.6` to `1`.
- **Assessment**: the detector calls bounce easing dated. On a counter
  bubble a slight overshoot is a deliberate, legible gesture. Worth a
  decision, not a fix.
- **Recommendation**: keep, or move to `ease-out-quint` if the brand should
  read as strictly composed. Owner's call.

### [P3] Desktop text links are under 24px tall

- **Location**: nav and footer links at ≥1024px
- **Category**: Responsive
- **Measured**: 14 targets below 44×44 at 1440px, all of them text links
  15–23px tall. At 390px: **0 targets under 44px**.
- **Assessment**: SC 2.5.8 asks for 24×24 with a spacing exception these
  links satisfy — nav items sit 40px apart. Recorded for completeness.

## Patterns

No systemic problems. The two theming findings are single-value gaps in an
otherwise complete token system, and the two performance findings are the
same pattern (`transition: height`) in two places rather than a habit
spread through the file.

## Positive findings

Verified, not assumed:

- **Every text token clears WCAG AA** against the brightest part of the
  animated background, measured across 10.4M background pixels:
  `--beige` 9.30, `--ink` 9.26, `--gold` 8.60, `--gold-dim` 5.49,
  `--ink-dim` 4.62, `--ink-faint` 4.62.
- **19 form controls, 0 unlabelled, 0 relying on a placeholder alone.**
- **30 interactive elements, 0 without an accessible name.**
- One `h1`, no skipped heading levels, `lang="en"`, a working skip link.
- Tab order follows the visual order and every stop shows a 2px outline.
- The one `target="_blank"` carries `rel="noopener"`.
- Five images, none missing `alt`; the single empty `alt` is decorative and
  correct.
- **Zero touch targets under 44px on mobile.** Zero horizontal overflow at
  390, 1024 and 1440.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured without a blanket `0.01ms` kill: the
  cell field freezes on the frame it reached and keeps its texture.
- No `will-change` anywhere — it is a targeted hint, and nothing here has
  earned it.
- The decorative WebGL layer is gated: no WebGL, Save-Data, 2g or under
  4 GB of memory and the 601 KB is never requested. LCP on slow 3G is
  unchanged whether it loads or not.
